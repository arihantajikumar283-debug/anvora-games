Our Story

Games that respect your time

Anvora Games is a small studio with one job: make browser games that feel premium without ever asking you to wait.

Who we are

Built by one developer, played by everyone

Anvora Games began as a simple idea: the best browser games shouldn't need an app store, an account wall, or a single megabyte of install. Founded by developer Arihant, the studio set out to prove that instant-play HTML5 games could look and feel as polished as anything you'd download.

Every title in the Anvora catalog is designed around three principles — load fast, run smoothly, and stay fun long after the novelty wears off. We build small, ship often, and treat every pixel like it belongs in a much bigger studio's flagship release.

Our mission is simple: create lightweight, high-quality browser games that work instantly, without downloads — so anyone, on any device, can go from a link to actually playing in under a second.

Speed
