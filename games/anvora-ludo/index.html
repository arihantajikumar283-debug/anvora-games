How To Play

  • Roll the dice on your turn. Rolling a 6 lets a token leave base and grants an extra roll.
  • Move a token the number of squares shown. If more than one token can legally move, tap the glowing token you want.
  • Land on an opponent's token to send it back to base — unless it's on a ★ safe cell or a colored start cell.
  • Bring all 4 tokens around the board into your colored home lane. You need the exact roll to enter the final square.
  • Three 6's in a row forfeits the extra turn, to keep things moving.
  • First player to get all 4 tokens home wins!
