NIRA

Traditional Kerala Strategy Game

Difficulty:
A Traditional Game from Kerala, India

Rooted in the traditional games of Kerala, India, Nira is a two-player alignment game where every move matters. Place your pieces, move along connected paths, and outsmart your opponent to form a line of three.

How to play

Each player has 3 pieces. Take turns placing them on the 9 points (nobody can win while placing). Then take turns sliding one piece along a drawn line to an empty neighbouring point. Get your three pieces in a straight line — row, column or diagonal — to win. Orange goes first.
