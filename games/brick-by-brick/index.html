How to Play

Goal

  • Keep the ball alive with your paddle and destroy every brick.
  • Chain destructions without losing the ball to build your combo multiplier.
  • Catch falling power-ups for temporary boosts.

Desktop Controls

  • Move paddle — Mouse movement, A / D, or Left / Right arrow keys
  • Launch ball — Space
  • Pause — Esc

Mobile Controls

  • Move paddle — drag your finger anywhere on the arena
  • Or use the ◀ ▶ touch buttons
  • Launch ball — tap LAUNCH
  • Pause — tap ❚❚
