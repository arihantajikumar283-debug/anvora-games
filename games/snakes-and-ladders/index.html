How to Play

1. Roll the dice.
2. Your token moves that many squares.
3. Land on a ladder bottom → climb up.
4. Land on a snake head → slide down.
5. You must roll the exact number to land on 100 — an overshoot skips your turn.
6. First to reach 100 wins!
