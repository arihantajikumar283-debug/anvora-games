HOW TO PLAY

Desktop:
A / ← — move left
D / → — move right
W / ↑ — front / move forward
S / ↓ — back / slow down
SPACE — optional boost

Mobile:
Bottom-left: LEFT / RIGHT to steer.
Bottom-right: FRONT / BACK to drive.
You can hold LEFT or RIGHT together with FRONT to turn while accelerating.

Avoid crashing into traffic. Survive as long as you can to rack up score!
