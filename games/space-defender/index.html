Instructions

Desktop Controls

  • Move — Arrow Keys or WASD
  • Fire — Space Bar (hold for sustained fire)
  • Special Ability (Star Strike) — E
  • Pause — P or Escape

Mobile Controls

  • Move — On-screen D-Pad (bottom-left) or drag/swipe on screen
  • Fire — FIRE button (bottom-right)
  • Special Ability — ★ button next to Fire
  • Pause — ❚❚ button (top-right of HUD)

Tips

  • Chain kills quickly to build your combo multiplier — it boosts score.
  • Fill the ★ meter by destroying enemies, then unleash Star Strike to clear the screen.
  • Spend coins in Upgrades between runs to permanently boost your ship.
  • Watch for the boss warning banner — a commander is inbound!
