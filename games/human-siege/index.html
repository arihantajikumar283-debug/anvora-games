HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Press and hold directly on the hero's projectile, then drag it backward in any direction to aim.

🚀 Pull farther for more power. Let go to launch instantly!

🧱 Smash wooden, stone and metal structures to expose the Iron Crew's guards.

💥 Defeat every guard before you run out of shots.

⭐ Destroy more of the base and save shots for extra stars.

📱 Touch: press the projectile, drag, release. 🖥 PC: click the projectile, drag, release. R = restart, ESC = pause.
