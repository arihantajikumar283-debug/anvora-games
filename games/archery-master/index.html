How to Play

Aim: move your mouse (or drag your finger) to aim the bow. The crosshair shows where you're pointing.

Draw & shoot — PC: click and hold to draw the bow, then release to fire. Hold longer for more power.

Draw & shoot — Mobile: touch and drag anywhere to aim and draw, then lift your finger to shoot — or use the large SHOOT button in the corner.

Scoring: hit the bullseye for the highest score. Inner rings score less the farther out you hit. Chain hits without missing to build a combo multiplier.

Wind & moving targets: later levels add wind (aim slightly against it) and targets that move — time your shot as they cross.

Difficulty: Easy gives you big, forgiving targets. Nightmare demands precise, well-timed shots against small, fast, wind-blown targets.
