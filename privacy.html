Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Overview

Anvora Games ("we", "our", "us") operates this website and the games hosted on it. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit, and how we use it. We built Anvora Games to be lightweight and low-friction, and that philosophy extends to how we handle data — we collect as little as possible.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact details you voluntarily submit through our contact form (name, email, message).
  • Basic, anonymized usage data such as pages visited and general device type, used to improve performance.
  • Local gameplay data (such as high scores) stored only in your browser's local storage — this never leaves your device.

3. How We Use Information

Contact form submissions are used solely to respond to your message. Anonymized usage data helps us understand which games and pages perform well, so we can prioritize improvements. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

4. Cookie Policy

Anvora Games uses minimal cookies and browser storage — primarily to remember your theme preference (dark or light mode) and, where applicable, in-game high scores. These are functional and do not track you across other websites. You can clear them at any time through your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use privacy-conscious analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns. These services do not receive personally identifying information from us.

6. Your Choices

You may disable cookies in your browser at any time, though some features — like remembering your preferred theme — may stop working as a result. You can also request that we delete any information submitted via our contact form by emailing us.

7. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. Continued use of Anvora Games after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact page.
